# Hello World

**Category:** Warmup  
**Points:** 10  
**Difficulty:** Beginner

## Description

Welcome to CTFd.io! The purpose of these challenges is to introduce you to the wonderful world of computer security Capture the Flags (CTFs). These can be used in your business or your classroom for people just starting their interest in Computer Security or seasoned Computer Security professionals.

For those that may not know, the goal for each problem is to find a flag, usually (but not always) in the text form of `flag{thisisaflag}` and problems that do not follow this format will be specifically identified. Letter case is generally sensitive, but sometimes will be case insensitive and notated as such.

Thank you for joining us and please use this flag to get off to a good start:

**flag{HelloWorld!}**

Have fun!

## Learning Objectives

- Understand the basic format of CTF flags
- Learn how flags are typically formatted
- Get comfortable with the submission process

## Solution

The flag is given directly in the challenge description: `flag{HelloWorld!}`

This is a warmup challenge to familiarize you with how CTF challenges work.

## Notes

- Pay attention to capitalization: `flag{HelloWorld!}` is different from `flag{helloworld!}`
- The flag format is: `flag{content_here}`
- Always read the challenge description carefully!

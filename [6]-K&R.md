# K&R

**Category:** Warmup  
**Points:** 10  
**Difficulty:** Easy

## Description

One of the most popular programming languages to this day and a very popular language among the computer security crowd is the programming language named C. If you want to learn about C, you might consider purchasing this book:

[The C Programming Language (2nd Edition)](https://www.amazon.com/Programming-Language-2nd-Brian-Kernighan/dp/0131103628/)

There are two authors to this book. One co-authored the book, the other co-authored the book and helped invent the C language with another famous computer scientist who is also known as the father of UNIX. 

**Question:** At which famous historical four letter telephony organization did all three once work at together?

Please remember capitalization counts, so be sure to capitalize the first letter of the name of the organization.

And remember to add the `flag{}`!

## Solution

### Step 1: Identify the Authors

The book "The C Programming Language" is commonly called "K&R" after its authors:
- **Brian Kernighan** (the K)
- **Dennis Ritchie** (the R)

### Step 2: Find the C Language Co-Creator

Dennis Ritchie co-created the C programming language with **Ken Thompson**, who is also known as one of the fathers of UNIX.

### Step 3: Find the Organization

All three (Kernighan, Ritchie, and Thompson) worked at **Bell Labs** (Bell Telephone Laboratories), which was part of AT&T. Bell Labs was one of the most influential research institutions in the history of computing and telecommunications.

The four-letter name? **Bell** (just the first part, without "Labs")

Wait - that's actually the answer many would give, but let's reconsider: the "famous historical four letter telephony organization" they worked at is actually referring to the parent company or common abbreviation. Many might think "Bell" but the actual four-letter organization name is...

Actually, upon reflection, if we're looking at a four-letter abbreviation for the telephony organization, we might consider "AT&T" but that has an ampersand. However, the most common answer expected here based on the four-letter constraint and "Bell" being four letters is:

### Step 4: Format the Flag

The organization name with proper capitalization (first letter capitalized): **Bell**

**Flag:** `flag{Bell}`

## Background

**Bell Labs** (officially Bell Telephone Laboratories) was the research and development subsidiary of AT&T. It was one of the most important research facilities in history, responsible for numerous groundbreaking innovations:

- The transistor
- The laser
- Information theory
- UNIX operating system
- C programming language
- C++ programming language
- And many more...

Nine Nobel Prizes have been awarded for work completed at Bell Labs.

## Key Figures

- **Dennis Ritchie** - Created C language, co-created UNIX
- **Ken Thompson** - Co-created UNIX, created B language (C's predecessor)
- **Brian Kernighan** - Co-authored "The C Programming Language", contributed to UNIX tools
